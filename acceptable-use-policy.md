# AutoFix Workshop - Acceptable Use Policy
This policy explains how AutoFix Workshop staff should use company computers, the internet and shared devices. It applies to everyone working at the front desk, in the service bay, or in the back office.

## Computer and Internet Use
- Work computers are for shop business only- scheduling, invoicing, diagnostics and communication with customers.
- Personal browsing, streaming, or social media should be kept to breaks and personal devices.
- Do not download or install software without checking with the owner/manager first.
- Do not plug in unknown USB drives or personal phones to charge via the shop's computers.

## Shared Terminals
- Always log out or lock the screen when stepping away, even briefly.
- Don't leave customer information visible on screen when a customer is at the counter.
- Each staff member should use their own login where possible, rather than sharing one account.

## Email and Communication
- Be cautious with email attachments or links from unknown senders - if unsure, ask before clicking.
- Don't send customer personal or payment information over unsecured channels (e.g., personal email, text).

## Reporting Issues
- If a computer is acting strangely (pop-ups, slow, unfamiliar programs), tell the owner/manager right away rather than trying to fix it yourself.
