# AutoFix Workshop - Password and Access Control Policy
This policy sets basic rules for passwords and system access at AutoFix Workshop, to keep customer and business information safe while staying practical for a small team.

## Password Rules
- Passwords must be at least 8 characters, with a mix of letters and numbers.
- Don't use obvious passwords like "password123" or the shop's name.
- Never write passwords on sticky notes near a shared terminal.
- Change your password if you think someone else may know it.

## Account Access
- Each staff member should have their own login for the booking app and any shared system, rather than one shared account for everyone.
- Only the owner/manager has access to QuickBooks and payroll information.
- Mechanics only need access to the booking app to check vehicle/service details - not financial or customer payment records.

## Screen Locking (Shared Terminals)
- Always lock your screen when stepping away from the front desk PC or back office laptop, even for a minute.
- Don't leave a logged-in session unattended where a customer could walk up to it.

## Leaving the Company
- When a staff member leaves, their login access should be removed or the password changed the same day.

## Customer Keys and Physical Access
- Customer vehicle keys should be kept in the locked key cabinet at the front desk, not left on desks or in the service bay.
- Only staff on shift should have access to the key cabinet.
